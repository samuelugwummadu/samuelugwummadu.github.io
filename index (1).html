Case study in full: Zalando CSRD readiness

Methodology: independent desk review of Zalando SE's publicly filed 2025 combined non-financial declaration (ESRS/CSRD + EU Taxonomy). No non-public information used.

1. What's strong

  • Full ESRS 2 general disclosures (BP-1, BP-2) present, with explicit acknowledgment of estimation methods and their sources — good transparency practice
  • Science-based near-term targets (55% Scope 1+2 reduction by 2033 vs. 2022 baseline; 33% Scope 3 reduction for purchased goods/services and upstream transport) with a 2040/2050 net-zero ambition
  • Independent limited assurance already obtained (KPMG AG, Berlin) — ahead of many peers still preparing for mandatory assurance

2. Gaps / assurance risk areas

  • Scope 3 data quality: company discloses reliance on industry averages for categories with weak Tier 2/Tier 4 supplier visibility — a common CSRD audit finding and a defensible-but-flagged estimation risk
  • Customer use-phase emissions are modelled from external research rather than primary data — worth a stated data-improvement roadmap with milestones
  • 2025 report absorbs ABOUT YOU post-acquisition from the transaction date — integration of a new entity's ESG data is a classic year-one CSRD complexity point

3. If I were advising on next-cycle reporting

Prioritise a Tier 2/Tier 4 supplier data-collection roadmap with interim proxies clearly flagged (reduces future restatement risk), and build a specific acquisition-integration disclosure note for ABOUT YOU's first full reporting year to pre-empt assurance questions on consolidation boundaries.
